Game Collection: Demo

Overview:

Game Collection is an Android app to save and organize your video game collection, built as a study project in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose (Material 3).

Add games with cover, platform, genre and year, mark them as To Play, Playing or Completed, search by name, keep favourites and switch between light and dark mode — everything stays saved on the phone.

The challenge:

Problem: Keeping track of a game collection across different consoles usually ends up in scattered notes and lists.

Solution: A simple offline Android app that keeps every game in one place, with covers, status, search and favourites.
